Market overview

Data: biquote public market candle API, subject to availability and provider terms. Forex, gold and Bitcoin prices may differ from JustMarkets or other venues and may be delayed or unavailable. Every candle and setup is evaluated only after the relevant M1/M5 bars have closed. Quote snapshots are typically checked every 10 seconds; completed candles update about once a minute when the tab is active. Forex and Gold usually trade 24/5, while Bitcoin quotes can be available on weekends. Safari may pause updates in the background. These third-party indicative prices are not tradeable broker quotes. No orders, push alerts, brokerage access, SL/TP execution, accuracy promise, or financial recommendations. Use this as personal educational research; the feed is supplied by a third party and is not guaranteed.
